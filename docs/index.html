How it works

ApKilla uses an Accessibility service to open the current app's system information and tap the built-in Force stop controls. Android Settings may appear briefly.

Common questions

Does this really force-stop an app?

It automates Android's standard Force stop UI. The result depends on the phone and app. A successful confirmation tap alone cannot independently prove that the package stopped.

Why does ApKilla need Accessibility?

Android does not allow a regular app to force-stop another through a public API. Accessibility lets ApKilla find and tap controls in Android's own Settings interface. Read the privacy explanation before enabling it.

Will it work on my phone?

Android 7.0+ is required, but manufacturers customize Settings and background service behavior. Consult the compatibility notes and report issues with your model and Android version.

Does it use the Internet or send usage data?

No Internet permission, account, analytics or telemetry. The foreground-app target is stored locally to perform the action. The v0.4.0 QA build also stores limited outcome history locally.
